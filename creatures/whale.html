Ocean Giants

Whales

Whales are the largest animals to have ever lived, navigating acoustic highways that span entire oceans. Their migrations fertilize phytoplankton blooms that produce most of the oxygen we breathe.

Interesting Facts

  • Sperm whales boast the largest brain on Earth—five times the mass of a human brain.
  • Humpbacks fast for months during migration, living off blubber reserves before feasting in polar seas.
  • Each whale fall feeds deep-sea ecosystems for decades, fueling biodiversity from the surface to the abyss.
