Reef Roommates

Clownfish

Clownfish brighten coral reefs with bold stripes and bold personalities. Nestled within the stinging tentacles of sea anemones, they trade protection for a steady stream of food scraps.

Interesting Facts

  • All clownfish hatch as males and can transition to female when it's time to lead a new family group.
  • Their mucus coating shields them from anemone stings, allowing the partnership to flourish.
  • Clownfish fan their fins to oxygenate eggs, fiercely guarding nests from intruders.
